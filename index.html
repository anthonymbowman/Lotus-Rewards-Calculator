Private LP Rewards Program

View program explanation

The private LP program has two reward phases and distributes rewards every two weeks. Your estimated rewards depend on your commitment size, total private LP pool size, and expected market conditions.

  • Pre-Deposit: rewards are allocated based on your share of the private LP pool.
  • Post-Launch: rewards are allocated based on your share of spread-interest earned.
  • Base APY: underlying yield assumption for the phase (pre-deposit fixed at 3.75%).
  • Incentive APY: annualized value of Lotus token rewards for that phase.
  • Total APY: Base APY plus Incentive APY.
